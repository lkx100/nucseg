    Part 6

    Where this leaves us

    Three points hold the whole project so far.

    1. One model change worked.
    Giving the model a full-size view of the pixels (run C) lifted the score from about 0.914 to 0.920.
    2. Nothing after it did.
    Nine more runs tried five training ideas and model averaging. All of them land between 0.919 and 0.923. Running the same thing twice already differs by 0.35 points.
    3. The missing points sit in few photos.
    The typical photo already scores 0.938. 47 of 603 photos pull the average down, and one wrong answer key explains three of them.

    Two decisions that are yours

    Run experiment 09?
    Train the current model again without the photo that has the blank answer key. It costs about 2.2 GPU-hours. My estimate is a gain of about 0.25 points. That is an estimate, not a result.
    Correct the blank answer key?
    It would recover about 0.15 points more. But it changes the answers we grade against, so I won't do it without your word.

    Even with both, about 1 point stays missing. It sits in the other 44 low-scoring photos. Nobody has looked at what the model drew on them yet. That is the next thing to look at, before any new model idea.
